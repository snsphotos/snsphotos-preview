Aloha!

Photography through my eyes.

I'm Stacey Noelani Spirz, a part-time photographer born and raised on the beautiful island of Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi. My love for photography began when I was young, and I've almost always had a camera by my side.

I consider myself an enthusiastic amateur, and I'm usually happiest behind the lens — whether I'm helping someone capture a special moment or simply seeing what catches my eye. I like to keep my photographs true to the moment, using small adjustments to lighting and color when needed.

My full-time work as a legal assistant and my part-time work as a Hawaiʻi notary keep me busy, but I cherish the time I get to spend with my camera. I especially enjoy sunny landscapes, peaceful evenings, fascinating animals, and the beauty of people and everyday life.

Open to photo requests. If you'd like me to photograph you, an event, your art, your business, or candid moments, I'd love to hear from you. I work on a donation basis, and I want you to be happy with the photographs we create together.

Mahalo!
